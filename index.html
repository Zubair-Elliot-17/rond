Your mug isn't an ornament. It's the first thing you hold every morning.

We throw, trim, glaze and fire everything in one room on Albert Road. Small batches, local clay, and a glaze book we've been filling since 2016.

Lump→Pot

Clay starts lumpy. We keep the wheel turning until it comes out round.

01

Wedge &
centre

We knead air out of the clay, then press it dead-centre on the wheel. Every wobble later starts here.

02

Throw &
pull

Three pulls lift the walls to height. A mug wall ends near 5 mm, so it's light in the hand but survives a dishwasher.

03

Trim &
glaze

At leather-hard we carve the foot, bisque at 1000 °C, then dip in glazes we mix by the gram.

04

Fire &
finish

Glaze firing to cone 6, about 1222 °C, then a slow cool overnight. We sand every foot before it leaves.

    FAQ.txt
    I've never touched clay. Can I join?
    Yes. The Taster evening is built for first-timers. You leave with two pieces, fired and posted two weeks later.
    Are the pots food safe?
    All our glazes are lead-free and fired to cone 6. Dishwasher and microwave are fine; the oven isn't.
    Do you take commissions?
    For restaurants and hotels, from 40 pieces. Lead time is about ten weeks, because every batch fires twice.
    Can I fire my own work here?
    Members get shelf space in every firing. Non-members pay R4 per cubic centimetre of kiln space.
